`OrbitSymbol`, the symbol of Orbit alone: the orbit in `currentColor` and the satellite in brand orange, the same geometry as the "o" of `OrbitWordmark` (brand assets v1.0). Square, sized with `size-*`, decorative. For a place where the name stands in words beside it, such as a button "Inloggen met Orbit" next to GitHub's mark. Additive.
