`Chart`: `axis="category"` names every bar (spaces, branches) and lays the bars down, one row per name with its sum, when the names do not fit under them; `step` sets the grid step in the unit of the values (60 for seconds read as minutes); `loadingCount` sets how many soft columns stand in while loading. Labels of a time axis that no step ends on are counted back from the last point, so the rhythm holds to the end. The grid of an empty chart stops short of its sentence, the words at the end of a forecast keep clear of the grid lines, and the dark skeleton is a step brighter. `Sparkline`: the period before is a 1px dashed trace at 40% of the line's ink. Additive.
