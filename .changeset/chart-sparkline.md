`Chart` and `Sparkline`, drawn as our own SVG from the tokens. `Chart` is a bar chart (stacked, in the order of `series`) or a line chart (`kind="line"`) over `points` with one value per series (`null` leaves a gap; a point may carry a `title` for its tooltip): a quiet hairline grid with its values at the left (`format`; whole steps when every value is a whole number), the labels of the points under it (every few when they would collide, always the last, the first and last kept inside the plot), a legend for two series or more, and a tooltip that flips and clamps to stay inside the plot. Bars take about half their band (at most 40px) with a 2px rounded end and a square foot; the parts of a stacked bar are 2px apart, a part above zero shows at least 3px, and a status part (`danger`) is striped as well as tinted, in the legend too. Lines are 2px in the full ink (`--nt-chart-line`) from edge to edge; a single line lies on a wash, a `reference` series (a pace) is dashed, and `limit` draws a level to stay under, named at its start. `total` adds the sum of a stacked bar to its tooltip. The plot is one tab stop named by `label`: the arrow keys, Home and End read the points with the same tooltip and a polite announcement, Escape closes it; a tap reads a point on a touch screen and a second tap closes it. The drawing is `aria-hidden`; a screen reader also gets the values as a table named by `label` (`none` for a point without a value). `loading` shows soft shapes and makes the figure busy; `empty` (and `emptyNext`, the next step) puts words in a grid without values, without a legend. `Sparkline` is the course of a figure the height of a word, for a `StatTile`, with a dot on the latest value. New tokens `--nt-chart-mark`, `--nt-chart-line`, `--nt-chart-wash`, `--nt-chart-skeleton` and `--nt-chart-reference` in both themes; `--nt-chart-ground` may be set by a host for the ring around a dot. `StatTile` lets its comparison wrap under the trend instead of cutting it. Additive.
