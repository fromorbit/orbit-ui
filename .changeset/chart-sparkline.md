`Chart` and `Sparkline`, drawn as our own SVG from the tokens. `Chart` is a bar chart (stacked, in the order of `series`) or a line chart (`kind="line"`) over `points` with one value per series (`null` leaves a gap): a quiet hairline grid with its values at the left (`format`), the labels of the points under it (every few when they would collide), a legend for two series or more, and a tooltip under the pointer. Bars are at most 24px wide with a 4px rounded end and a square foot, the parts of a stacked bar 2px apart; lines are 2px with round joins, a single line lies on a wash, and a `reference` series (a plan, a limit) is dashed. Marks take the new `--nt-chart-mark` (a step quieter than the accent; the bar under the pointer turns to the accent) and status tones only where a series means that status. The drawing is `aria-hidden`; a screen reader gets the values as a table named by `label`. `loading` shows soft shapes and makes the figure busy; `empty` puts one sentence in a plot without values. `Sparkline` is the course of a figure the height of a word, for a `StatTile`, with a dot on the latest value. New tokens `--nt-chart-mark`, `--nt-chart-wash` and `--nt-chart-reference` in both themes; `--nt-chart-ground` may be set by a host for the ring around a dot. Additive.
