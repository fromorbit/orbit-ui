`ContextMenu`, the menu a right click opens on a region (a row, a card, a file): a long press on a touch screen and the context-menu key open it too. It takes the item model of `DropdownMenu` (`DropdownMenuItem`, `DropdownMenuHeader`, `checks`, `density`, `height`) and draws its rows with the same code and in the same frame, so one array can feed both the `⋯` of a row and a right click on it. The region keeps its own looks and carries `data-state="open"` while its menu is up, so a host can keep it lit; `disabled` leaves the right click to the browser. Non-modal, like `DropdownMenu`. Shift+F10 and the context-menu key open it on what holds the focus in the region on every platform (macOS does not turn them into a right click), except in a field. It closes when a scroll moves its region, slides back inside the window across when a press in the middle of a phone leaves room on neither side, wraps the arrow keys at its ends, and the release of the press that opened it (a long press) never chooses the row under it.

`DropdownMenu`: a row without an icon, in a panel where another row has an icon, a glyph or a check at the start, keeps an empty slot of 14px, so every label of the panel starts on one line (the `Open` story: "Archiveren" now lines up with "Hernoemen"). A panel without any icon is unchanged.

Both menus, through the rows they share: a shortcut is plain quiet text at the end of its row, without a cap, and it is hidden under a coarse pointer, where rows grow to 44px. A radio row shows a dot instead of the check of a switch. A radio group right under a `heading` row takes its name from that heading (`aria-labelledby`), and a heading with no glyph starts on the column of the labels. A row the keyboard walked to stands on `surface-active` (a danger row on twice its own tint), a step of tint rather than a ring. A `disabled` row with a `hint` shows that reason in sight, as a quiet 12px line under the row on the column of the labels, which also describes it; the tooltip is gone from such a row. A count stands in a column of its own before the shortcut, so the counts of a menu line up. A submenu is capped at 256px (was 320px) and, when neither side has room, slides back over its parent to stay 8px inside the window. The panel is capped at 320px or the width of the window less 16px, a submenu at 256px or the room Radix measured, so a long label ends in an ellipsis and stays inside the window. Under a head with a glyph, the rows lead with a 30px slot, so the head's title and the row labels share one left edge. A `danger` row keeps its icon in its own tone in `comfortable` density too. The line under a head is drawn, not announced as a separator.
