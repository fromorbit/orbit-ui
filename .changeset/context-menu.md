`ContextMenu`, the menu a right click opens on a region (a row, a card, a file): a long press on a touch screen and the context-menu key open it too. It takes the item model of `DropdownMenu` (`DropdownMenuItem`, `DropdownMenuHeader`, `checks`, `density`, `height`) and draws its rows with the same code and in the same frame, so one array can feed both the `⋯` of a row and a right click on it. The region keeps its own looks and carries `data-state="open"` while its menu is up, so a host can keep it lit; `disabled` leaves the right click to the browser. Non-modal, like `DropdownMenu`.

`DropdownMenu`: a row without an icon, in a panel where another row has an icon, a glyph or a check at the start, keeps an empty slot of 14px, so every label of the panel starts on one line (the `Open` story: "Archiveren" now lines up with "Hernoemen"). A panel without any icon is unchanged.

Both menus, through the rows they share: a shortcut is plain quiet text at the end of its row, without a cap, and it is hidden under a coarse pointer, where rows grow to 44px. A radio row shows a dot instead of the check of a switch. A radio group right under a `heading` row takes its name from that heading (`aria-labelledby`), and a heading with no glyph starts on the column of the labels. A row the keyboard walked to gets an inset 1px ring in `border-focus`. The panel is capped at 320px or the width of the window less 16px, a submenu at 320px or the room Radix measured, so a long label ends in an ellipsis and stays inside the window. Under a head with a glyph, the rows lead with a 30px slot, so the head's title and the row labels share one left edge. A `danger` row keeps its icon in its own tone in `comfortable` density too. The line under a head is drawn, not announced as a separator.
