`PlanCard`: a plan of a subscription as a card. The name (15px/600) and the price (20px, tabular digits) with what it is for beside it, an optional sentence on who the plan is for, the features after a check (each may carry a quiet figure at its end, such as "182 gebruikt"), whatever the caller adds, and a foot with a muted summary and one action. An `<article>` named by the plan on the plane of a card (`bg-overlay`, 12px, no frame); `current` with `currentLabel` marks the plan the workspace is on with a tag beside its name, never with a frame or a fill. The card fills its cell and pins the foot, at least as high as a small button, to the bottom, so plans side by side line up their summaries and actions. Additive.
