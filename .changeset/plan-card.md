`PlanCard` and `PlanCards`: a plan of a subscription as a card, and plans side by side. A card holds the name (15px/600) and the price (20px, tabular digits) with what it is for beside it, an optional sentence on who the plan is for, the features after a check (each may carry a quiet figure at its end, such as "182 u gebruikt", read after a comma), whatever the caller adds, and a foot with a muted summary and one action at its end. An `<article>` named by the plan, on the plane of a card (`bg-overlay`, 12px, no frame) or, with `plane="surface"`, on the tint of a `SettingsGroup`. `current` with `currentLabel` marks the plan the workspace is on by a stronger tint and a tag inside its heading ("Team, Huidig plan"), never by a frame. The action is described by the plan's name and grows to 40px under a coarse pointer. `PlanCards` is a list whose columns follow its own width (never under 240px a card) and whose rows are shared through `subgrid`, so the features and the feet of every plan line up. Additive.
