`PlanCard`: a feature line can show its use against the limit. `used: { value, max }` draws a 4px meter under the line (`role="meter"`, named by the label, saying the figure, so the visible figure is heard once), and `over` marks the one line that goes past the plan: figure and meter in the tone of attention, its words under the meter. The figure at the end of a line now drops under its label when the line has no room for both, instead of squeezing the label. Among other plans the current plan carries no action, so one decision has one button. The gallery story adds an over-limit plan, a plan to move to, two plans side by side and a card with many lines and a long name. Additive.
