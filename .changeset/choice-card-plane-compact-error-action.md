Light mode: a `ChoiceCard` is a white card with a hairline on the off-white page (`bg-plane shadow-plane`, the plane of a region), so a dimmed card fades frame and all and the clickable area reads as a card; in dark it is the same tint as before. The compact `ErrorState` (`size="sm"`) puts its action directly after the message instead of at the far edge of the row, so a framed "Opnieuw proberen" (`secondary`, `sm`) sits with what it answers rather than floating. The gallery stories of `SettingRow`, `ScrollArea`, `ResizeHandle`, `HostedSurface` and `PierreTree` stand on the same white plane in light.
