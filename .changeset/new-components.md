"@fromorbit/ui": minor
---

Twenty-six new components, the gaps against shadcn/ui that Orbit's and Halo's screens need. Each is additive and follows the rhythm of the kit (tokens for heights, radii, spacing and motion; the five type sizes; regions by tint).

- `FormField` and `Form`, the form system. `FormField` puts the label above its control with a description or an error below it, and hands the control `id`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid` and `aria-required` to spread (`{(field) => <Input label="…" {...field} />}`); the error replaces the description, carries a warning sign and is announced. `Form` switches the browser's own checks off and moves the focus to the first invalid field after a submit. `Input` gains `prefix` and `suffix` (text inside the frame, read as part of the description), and `Input`, `Textarea` and `Select` draw a danger border while `aria-invalid`; `Select` also takes `id`, `aria-invalid` and `aria-required`. Additive.
- `CheckboxField` (a box with its words beside it, clickable as a whole, with a description or an announced error) and `CheckboxGroup` (several boxes under a `<legend>`, the new value in the order of the options). `Checkbox` takes `checked="indeterminate"` (a dash, `aria-checked="mixed"`, a press turns it on) and `id`, `aria-invalid` and `aria-required`. Additive.
- `Combobox` and `MultiCombobox`: a choice out of a long list, found by typing. A button trigger like `Select`'s (it takes the props of `FormField` and `Field`, and `block` for a form column) opens a panel with a search field on top (the ARIA combobox, with `aria-activedescendant`) and a listbox below; the search ignores case and accents and matches every word in the label, `detail` or `keywords` (`filterComboboxOptions`). The multiple one toggles rows, keeps the panel open and lists the chosen labels or a count. New `COMBOBOX_PANEL` in the overlay frames. Additive.
- `Slider`: one number on a range (Radix), with the value in words for a screen reader (`valueText`, also shown beside the track), `onValueCommit` for a value that is costly to apply, and the props of `FormField` and `Field` on its thumb. Additive.
- `OtpInput`: a one-time code in separate boxes. One invisible `<input>` lies over the boxes, so pasting "123 456" fills them all, the browser offers a code from a message (`autocomplete="one-time-code"`), Backspace works and a screen reader meets one field; `onComplete` fires once when the last box fills. Digits or letters and digits (`cleanOtp`), `groupAfter` for a gap, and the props of `FormField`. Additive.
- `CreditCardField` and `CreditCardVisual`: the fields of a payment card (number, expiry, code, name) with a picture of the card that fills in as one types. The number groups itself per brand (4-4-4-4, 4-6-5 for Amex) and names its brand (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, or a generic card) from its first digits; the expiry writes its own slash; the caret stays behind the digit typed. Every field carries its `autocomplete` (`cc-number`, `cc-exp`, `cc-csc`, `cc-name`) and a number keyboard. Problems (length, Luhn, an expired or impossible month, the code's length per brand, a missing name) show through `FormField` once a field is left, or all at once with `showErrors`. The picture is decorative, shows at most the last four digits, wears the night gradient and Orbit's symbol, uses word marks rather than the networks' logos, and turns to its back while the code has the focus (a cut under `prefers-reduced-motion`). The rules are pure functions in `credit-card` (`cardBrandOf`, `formatCardNumber`, `luhnValid`, `formatExpiry`, `cardProblems`, `maskedCardNumber`). Presentation only: nothing is sent anywhere. Additive.
- `Calendar` and `DatePicker`, without a date library. `Calendar` is a month of day buttons with the keys of the ARIA date picker (arrows, Page Up/Down with Shift for a year, Home/End), one tab stop, month and weekday names from `Intl` in the provider's locale, `min`, `max` and `isDisabled` (such days stay reachable but cannot be chosen), and today marked. Days are `YYYY-MM-DD` (`IsoDate`), so no zone ever shifts a day; `addDays`, `addMonths`, `monthGrid`, `isIsoDate` and `todayIso` are exported. `DatePicker` opens it under a trigger that shows the day in the locale and takes the props of `FormField`. `Icon` gains `calendar` and `upload`. Additive.
- `FileDrop` (a zone to drop files on or click for the dialog, one button for the keyboard, a drop checked against `accept` with `acceptsFile`: what fits to `onFiles`, the rest to `onReject`, and an announced error) and `ProgressBar` (`role="progressbar"`, a known value or an indeterminate pulse that stands still under reduced motion). `FileInput` takes `accept` and `single`. Additive.
- `DataTable` and `Pagination`. `DataTable` is a real `<table>` with sortable headers (a button in the `<th>`, `aria-sort`, stable sort in the locale's order with `sortRows`, or a sort held by the caller), a column of row boxes with a select-all box that shows mixed, amounts aligned to the end on tabular figures, placeholder rows and `aria-busy` while loading, an empty slot across every column, columns that step aside below a width of the table itself (`@container`), and a list of cards for a narrow table (`card`). `Pagination` is a named `nav` with the count, the pages around the current one (`pageItems`: never a gap of one page, seven places at most), `aria-current="page"`, and an optional page size. Additive.
- `DataTable` takes `canSelect` (a row without a box, outside select-all), `current` (the row open beside the table, with the active fill and `aria-current`) and `onRowClick`; `TimelineItem` takes `tone` (`danger`, or `pending` for a dashed round). Additive.
- `StatTile` (the card a dashboard starts with: label, the figure large on tabular digits, a trend and what it is compared with, a named group), `TrendBadge` (an arrow for the direction and a colour for the verdict, two props because fewer failures is down and good, with a description for a screen reader) and `AvatarGroup` (overlapping faces with "+n", one named image, a ring in the colour of the plane behind it). Additive.
- `Sheet`: a panel along the right (or left) edge of the window, over the page, for the details of a row, a log or a filter. The layer of `Dialog` (modal, focus kept inside and handed back) with a header, a body that scrolls and a footer that stays; unlike a `Dialog` a click beside it closes it. The panel itself takes the focus on opening. New frames `SHEET_PANEL_END` and `SHEET_PANEL_START`. Additive.
- `Accordion` (several folds, one or any number open, Radix: titles in headings, arrows between them, a closed fold out of the document, an opened one fades in), `Timeline` with `TimelineItem` (events on one line in an ordered list, a decorative marker and a time), and `ShareBar` (how a whole splits into parts: a legend in words that a screen reader reads and one shared bar in status colours, with an optional sign per part; the generic form of the review's severity bar). Additive.
- `Select` takes `block` (the width of a form column, as `Combobox` and `DatePicker`); a `DataTable` column takes `align: 'center'` (a column of boxes); a task item of `Prose` hangs its wrapped lines beside its box. Additive.
