`FindingCard` and `SeverityBar`, the findings of a review. `FindingCard` is one finding as an `<article>` named by its weight and title ("Blokkade: Betalen met iDEAL loopt vast") and described by its place, on the card plane (`bg-overlay`, 12px, no frame): the sign of its weight in a column of its own beside the title (13px/500, a row-like card), the weight's word and the place in a 12px line under it (`locationLabel` puts a quiet "pagina" or "bestand" before it; a long path is cut in the middle so the file and line stay, the whole path as its title), what was seen in 13px `secondary` on a 20px line, then an optional row of `actions`, raised to 32px while the card is narrow and to 40px on a touch screen. `media` (a screenshot) is a 144×96 thumbnail that stands at the right while the card is wide enough and under the words, at the same size and before the actions, when it is not (a container query on the card); a long place is cut in the middle on a wide card and wraps on a narrow one. Long words break anywhere. `headingLevel` sets the level of the title. `SeverityBar` counts findings per weight in a `status` region as a `ShareBar` heavy to light with the same signs and colours (`SEVERITY_SIGN`), the bar proportional to the counts, as a stacked bar is read; it leaves a weight of zero out, drops the bar when there are only remarks, and is one calm line with a check (`labels.none`) when there is nothing. New `FindingSeverity`, `FINDING_SEVERITIES` and `SeverityBarLabels`.

`ShareBar`: each share is a pill of its own with a 2px gap and no track behind it, so the bar never reads as a meter with an empty rest; neutral shares take the muted ink, attention shares the new `--nt-attention-fill` (the amber itself in dark, a clear `#d9952b` in light, where the AA ochre of words looked muddy as a fill; Tailwind `attention-fill`); the legend's words are quiet labels in the secondary ink, the colour on the sign and the bar; a share is at least 12px wide, so a small one stays a piece of the bar; under forced colours the pills draw in `CanvasText`; new `hideBar`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `theme.css` sets `--nt-duration-fast` and `--nt-duration` to 0, so hover and press transitions stand still too. Additive.
