`Scrubber`: the timeline of a recording. One unbroken 2px track (the played part in the foreground colour, the rest in muted, each at least 3:1 against the other and the plane), a neutral playhead to drag or move with the keys (a 24px stem with a 16px knob; Radix's slider: one tab stop, arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End; a press anywhere seeks), a mark per event in the shape of its kind (`dot` 8px, `chevron`, `diamond`, `ring` 12px, each set off the line by a 1px edge of the plane) and red or amber with the soft glow of the status marks for what went wrong, round times underneath (`scrubberTicks`, thinned as the track narrows, with the end time always closing the row) and an optional legend that keeps every kind's word and count in sight, wrapping as it needs. Markers that would crowd (closer than 24px) are one mark (`scrubberGroups`): led by what went wrong and drawn at its moment, then by the rarest quiet kind, with a second kind that went wrong beside it on the centre line and a faint copy of the shape behind it when it holds more; no count is printed on the track, the hover card counts it (`groupLabel`). Each mark is a 24px pointer target, the louder one on top where two touch: a press seeks to it and resting on it lists every event in it. Where the playhead stands on a mark its knob gives way and the mark lies over the stem in its own tone. `controls` sets the player's transport right under the times, above the legend. `valueText` receives the markers the playhead stands on, so the slider can name them. `adjacentMarker` finds the previous or next marker of given kinds. The icons `play`, `pause`, `rewind` and `fast-forward` join the registry for a player's transport. Additive.
