`Scrubber`: the timeline of a recording. One unbroken 2px track (the played part in the foreground colour, the rest in muted, each at least 3:1 against the other and the plane), a neutral playhead to drag or move with the keys (a 24px stem with a 16px knob; Radix's slider: one tab stop, arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End; a press anywhere seeks), a mark per event in the shape of its kind (`dot` 8px, `chevron`, `diamond`, `ring` 12px, each set off the line by a 1px edge of the plane) and red or amber with the soft glow of the status marks for what went wrong, round times underneath (`scrubberTicks`, thinned as the track narrows; `scrubberLabels` adds the end time to close the row, keeps every other label a round time with the finest step that fits, and drops the last round label where it would crowd the end time) and an optional legend that keeps every kind's word and count in sight, wrapping as it needs and a balanced two-column grid packed to the start below 480px. Markers that would crowd (closer than 24px) are one mark (`scrubberGroups`): led by what went wrong and drawn at its moment, then by the rarest quiet kind, with a second kind that went wrong 4px beside it on the centre line (before it at the very end); quiet company is not drawn and no count is printed on the track, the hover card lists and counts it (`groupLabel`, which receives the markers of the group as well, so its heading can name the span and what went wrong). Marks whose drawn shapes would come within 8px merge as well, so no two shapes touch at any width, and the track stands 8px in from each side so a mark at the start or end is drawn whole. Each mark is a 24px pointer target, the louder one on top where two touch: a press seeks to it and resting on it lists every event in it. Where the playhead stands on a mark its knob and stem give way to a short bar under the mark, so no line ever runs through a shape. A kind with `named: false` (a click whose own words say "Klikt op") is not prefixed with its word in the hover card; `scrubberEventText` writes a line the same way for a host's `valueText`. `ScrubberMark` draws a kind's shape on its own, for the list of events beside the player, so each term has one shape everywhere. `controls` sets the player's transport right under the times, above the legend. `valueText` receives the markers the playhead stands on, so the slider can name them. `adjacentMarker` finds the previous or next marker of given kinds. The icons `play`, `pause`, `rewind` and `fast-forward` join the registry for a player's transport. Additive.
