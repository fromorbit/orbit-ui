`Scrubber`: the timeline of a recording. A track with the played part (quiet, not the accent) and a quiet cap where the recording ends, a playhead to drag or move with the keys (Radix's slider: one tab stop, arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End; a press anywhere seeks), a mark per event in the shape of its kind (`dot`, `chevron`, `diamond`, `ring`, each at one optical size and cut out of its plane, so it reads on the played part too) and red or amber with the soft glow of the status marks for what went wrong, round times underneath (`scrubberTicks`, thinned as the track narrows, with the end time where it fits) and an optional legend with a count per kind. Markers that would touch are one mark (`scrubberGroups`): led by what went wrong and drawn at its moment, a second kind that went wrong stacked under it, and a count when it holds more. Each mark is a 24px pointer target: a press seeks to it and resting on it lists every event in it. Where the playhead stands on a mark, the thumb opens into a ring in that mark's tone, so the error stays in sight, and the time label under it steps aside. `valueText` receives the markers the playhead stands on, so the slider can name them. `legendActions` puts a control in the legend in place of a kind's count (stepping between the errors beside "Fout"). `adjacentMarker` finds the previous or next marker of given kinds. Additive.

`Slider`: the thumb is a dot in the foreground colour with a rim of the card plane and no shadow. It was the accent's foreground, which is Midnight in dark, so the thumb vanished into the track there.
