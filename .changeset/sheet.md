`Sheet`: a panel along the right (or left) edge of the window, over the page, for the details of a row, a log or a filter. The layer of `Dialog` (modal, focus kept inside and handed back) with a header, a body that scrolls and a footer that stays; unlike a `Dialog` a click beside it closes it. The panel itself takes the focus on opening. New frames `SHEET_PANEL_END` and `SHEET_PANEL_START`. Additive.
