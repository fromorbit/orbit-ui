`ChatMessage`, `ChatLog` and `Composer`, the pieces of a chat. `ChatMessage` is one turn as a named `<article>`: your own message a 12px bubble on the `bubble` plane against the right edge (its corner towards you squared), its actions beside it on hover or focus (always in sight on a touch screen); an answer plain text across the column on a 20px line, with its actions and an 11px meta under it. `streaming` makes it `aria-busy`, puts a quiet caret after the last paragraph and holds the actions back; `error` adds a line with a warning sign as an `alert` that also describes the turn, and `errorAction` puts the way on ("Opnieuw proberen") on that line. `ChatLog` is the column of turns: a polite `log`, so a new turn is announced. `Composer` is a growing field and its toolbar as one plane on the `composer` tint, one hairline and no shadow, with the kit's focus ring while the keyboard is in the field: tools on the left (a slot for the ghost "+", the model and the access level, the toolbar being the `toolbar` container a collapsing `PickerPill` answers to), the round 32px send button on the right, on the accent only when a press sends something (`idle` and `aria-disabled` otherwise), Stop (a square on the accent) in its place while `running`, and Escape in the field presses it. Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a line, an Enter that confirms an input method does neither; `disabledReason` puts the reason inside the plane, steps the rest back and keeps the field a read-only tab stop described by it, with `disabledAction` for the way out; `attachments` and `footer` are slots above and below. `Prose`: inline code breaks inside the column, a table column aligned with `---:` stands on the right in tabular figures under 12px headers, and a code block fades out at its scrolling edge, keeps its last character off that edge and shows its buttons on a touch screen. New `ComposerLabels`. Additive.
