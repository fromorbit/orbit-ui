`ChatMessage`, `ChatLog` and `Composer`, the pieces of a chat. `ChatMessage` is one turn as a named `<article>`: your own message a 12px bubble on the `bubble` plane against the right edge (its corner towards you squared), its actions beside it on hover or focus (always in sight on a touch screen); an answer plain text across the column on a 20px line, with its actions and an 11px meta under it. `streaming` makes it `aria-busy`, puts a quiet caret after the last paragraph and holds the actions back; `error` adds a line with a warning sign as an `alert` that also describes the turn, and `errorAction` puts the way on ("Opnieuw proberen") on that line. `ChatLog` is the column of turns: a polite `log`, so a new turn is announced. `Composer` is a growing field and its toolbar as one plane on the `composer` tint, one inset hairline (so one row is 88px, on the grid) and no shadow, with the kit's focus ring in place of the hairline while the keyboard is in the field: tools on the left (a slot for the ghost "+", the model and the access level, the toolbar being the `toolbar` container a collapsing `PickerPill` answers to), the round 28px send button on the right, of one size with the tools (8px padding, the row pulled 6px so the "+" lines up with the text), on the accent only when a press sends something (`idle` and `aria-disabled` otherwise), Stop (a square on the accent) in its place while `running`, and Escape in the field presses it. Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a line, an Enter that confirms an input method does neither; `disabledReason` puts the reason inside the plane, steps the rest back and keeps the field a read-only tab stop described by it, with `disabledAction` for the way out; `attachments` and `footer` are slots above and below. Stop carries a "Stop (Esc)" tooltip. `Prose`: inline code breaks after each `/` of a path (and anywhere only as a fallback), a table is as wide as its columns with 32px rows, a table column aligned with `---:` stands on the right in tabular figures under 12px headers, and a code block fades out over 40px at its right edge while a line runs on past it, keeps its last character off that edge and shows its buttons on a touch screen. New `ComposerLabels`. Additive.

`Chip` `size="sm"` is a 28px pill on the `pill` tint without a border, its cross in sight at rest, and new `truncate="middle"` keeps a file's extension and carries the whole name as the title. `Textarea` runs on a 20px line (a framed one stays 32px). `ProjectMark` is 16px with an 11px letter, and the small `Avatar` and `AvatarGroup` take 11px letters, the smallest size of the scale. The `Shimmer` band rests off the line for the first third of each turn.
